# Zone Overview

Ways Around uses seven zones: three associated with each player and one neutral center.

| Zone | Owner | Town | Size | Biome | Role |
|---|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Spawn-A / Spawn-B | Player | Starting town | 0.8 | Faction biome | Opening economy |
| Side-A / Side-B | Player | Side town | 0.7 | Same as linked Spawn | Outer pressure route |
| Center-A / Center-B | Neutral | No town | 0.7 | Shared non-Sand biome | Forward treasure |
| Center | Neutral | Neutral town | 0.8 | Sand | Main conflict zone |

The expected value order is Center, Center-A/B, Side-A/B, then Spawn-A/B. Each player can advance through a side route or directly into their linked treasure zone.
