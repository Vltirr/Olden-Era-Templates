# Topology and Connections

## Ground Connections

| From | To | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Spawn-A | Side-A | Outer-route entry |
| Spawn-A | Center-A | Direct treasure entry |
| Side-A | Center | Central approach |
| Side-A | Center-B | Cross-route approach |
| Center | Center-A | Central access |
| Center | Center-B | Central access |
| Side-B | Center | Central approach |
| Side-B | Center-A | Cross-route approach |
| Spawn-B | Center-B | Direct treasure entry |
| Spawn-B | Side-B | Outer-route entry |

## Route Roles

- The outer route begins in Spawn, passes through the player's Side town, and reaches the center or the opposing treasure zone.
- The direct route enters the linked Center-A/B treasure zone from Spawn, then approaches the center or the opposing side.
- Center connects to both treasure zones and is the main high-value conflict point.
- One-way portals return from contested zones to the corresponding Spawn; see [special objects](../07-special-objects.md).
