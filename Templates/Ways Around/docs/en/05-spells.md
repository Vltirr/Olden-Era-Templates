# Spells

## Starting Spells

Every starting hero receives these neutral spells at zero mana cost:

| Spell | In-game effect |
|---|---|
| Back to Town | Returns the casting hero to a controlled town. |
| Relocation | Lets the casting hero interact with a controlled town to transfer creatures. |

## Neutral Magic Restrictions

`Second Wind` remains the only normally available neutral control spell. The following are banned: Pocket Dimension, Gate of Light, Dimension Door, Shadowflight, and Town Portal. Town Portal is specifically banned so it cannot be learned from Celestial Spires or similar objects.
